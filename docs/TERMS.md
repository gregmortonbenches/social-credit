## What Social Credit is

An app for a household to share chores. You form a Collective with the people
you live with, tasks are assigned each week, and completing or failing them
moves a score. The credits are a game. They are not money, they have no value
outside the app, they cannot be bought, sold, transferred or redeemed for

## Your Collective

Anyone with your Collective's five-digit code can join it. Treat the code like a
door key: share it only with the people you actually live with, and be aware
that a five-digit code is short enough to be guessed by someone determined.

What you put into the app is visible to everyone else in your Collective. That
includes the written explanation you give when responding to a denouncement.

## Denouncements

The app lets you formally accuse another member of failing a task. This is a
game mechanic between people who have chosen to play it together, but it is
still an accusation about a real person, visible to your household, that costs
them credits.

Do not use it to harass, bully or abuse anyone. Do not write anything in a
denouncement explanation that is threatening, hateful, or about someone's
protected characteristics. You may withdraw a denouncement while it is
unanswered.
